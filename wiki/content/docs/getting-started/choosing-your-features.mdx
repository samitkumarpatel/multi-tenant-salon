---
title: "Choosing Your Features"
description: "What each toggleable salon feature does and how to turn it on or off."
---

Your admin panel lets you turn specific parts of the platform on or off depending on what your business needs. You manage this from the **Features** step on the **Edit Salon** page (the same step also appears during initial sign-up). Turning a feature on unlocks its own dedicated section in your admin panel's side menu; turning it off hides that section again.

Here's what each feature does:

## Website

Gives your salon a public-facing website — showing your services, team, opening hours, and contact details to anyone who visits your salon's web address. Appears in your sidebar as **Website**.

Learn more: [Your Salon Website](/en/docs/website/your-salon-website).

## Booking

Lets customers book appointments online. Turning this on adds a booking calendar to your admin panel (shown in the sidebar as **Bookings**), where you can see upcoming appointments, confirm or manage them, and assign them to staff.

Learn more: [Managing Bookings](/en/docs/bookings/managing-bookings).

## Dashboard

Turns on the front-desk dashboard — a simplified screen your active staff can use at the salon to check customers in, take walk-in sales, and handle day-to-day front-desk tasks, without needing full access to your admin panel.

Learn more: [What Is the Front Desk App](/en/docs/front-desk/what-is-the-front-desk-app).

## Membership

Lets you sell membership plans on your website — recurring plans your customers pay for on a schedule, with automatic renewal reminders and a QR code members can show at check-in.

Learn more: [Creating Membership Plans](/en/docs/membership/creating-membership-plans).

## Shop

Adds an online shop to your salon's website, so you can list and sell products (like retail hair and beauty products) directly to customers.

Learn more: [Setting Up Your Shop](/en/docs/shop/setting-up-your-shop).

## Analytics

Turns on a business analytics dashboard, showing you a summary of visits and activity on your salon's public website — total page views, clicks, and your most visited pages.

Learn more: [Business Analytics](/en/docs/insights/business-analytics).

## Loyalty

Reserves a **Loyalty Program** section in your sidebar for a customer loyalty and rewards program.

<Callout type="info">
Loyalty Program is marked **coming soon** in the admin panel. Turning it on adds the section to your sidebar, but it doesn't unlock any working tools yet — there's nothing else to set up for it at the moment.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
You can turn features on and off at any time — nothing is lost when you turn a feature off, so it's safe to experiment and adjust as your business grows.
</Callout>
