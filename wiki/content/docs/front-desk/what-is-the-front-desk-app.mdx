If you work the till, greet walk-ins, or manage appointments during the day, you probably don't need (or want) access to your salon's full admin settings. That's what the **Dashboard** — also called the **front desk app** — is for.

## A separate app, built for the till

Your salon actually has two apps:

- **The admin app** is where the owner sets up the salon: business details, pricing, staff records, payment setup, and which features are switched on.
- **The Dashboard (front desk app)** is where day-to-day work happens: checking the schedule, taking walk-ins, ringing up sales, and checking in members.

The Dashboard has its own web address and its own sign-in — it's a separate app on purpose, so the person running the till during a busy afternoon isn't digging through admin settings to find what they need, and isn't able to accidentally change something they shouldn't.

## Turning it on for your salon (for owners)

Before any staff member can sign in to the Dashboard, you need to switch it on and choose what it can do — both steps happen in your own admin app, not the front desk app.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open your admin app and go to **Features**. Find **Dashboard** in the list ("Run daily appointments, checkout, and customer communication from one workspace") and switch it on. If you also want your front desk to manage appointments, make sure **Booking** is switched on too — appointment management on the desk depends on it.
</Step>
<Step>
Go to the salon's **Dashboard** settings page. Here you choose which tools the front desk is allowed to use, each with its own on/off switch:
- **Appointment management** — create, edit, confirm, cancel, and complete bookings. This switch stays off and greyed out until the Booking feature itself is switched on.
- **Cashier** — take in-salon payments for services and, if your webshop is set up, Shop products.

Turn on whichever your front desk should have, then select **Save settings**.
</Step>
<Step>
If you offer memberships, switch on the **Membership** feature from the same Features screen. There's no separate toggle for it on the Dashboard settings page — once Membership is on for the salon, member check-in and membership sales simply appear on the front desk.
</Step>
<Step>
Still on the Dashboard settings page, use **Payment setup** to connect card payments (so staff can take card payments at the till or on a card reader), and **Open Dashboard** to see the front desk app yourself before handing it to staff.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
Changes you make here take effect on the front desk app right away — there's nothing for staff to refresh or reinstall.
</Callout>

## What you can do here

Once the front desk is switched on and configured, the Dashboard gives whoever is staffing it:

- A view of today's appointments (if appointment management is on)
- A till/point-of-sale screen to ring up services and products, and an invoices screen to look back at past sales (if the cashier is on)
- Membership check-in and membership sales (if Membership is on for the salon)
- A quick look at salon info and payment status
