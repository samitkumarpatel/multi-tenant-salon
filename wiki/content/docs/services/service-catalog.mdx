---
title: "Building Your Service Menu"
description: "How to add, edit, and remove the services your salon offers, with pricing, duration, category, and the staff who can perform them"
---

Your service menu is what customers see when they book an appointment online — it's the list of treatments you offer, what they cost, how long they take, and (optionally) who on your team can perform them. You manage this list from the **Services** page in your salon admin dashboard.

## Adding your very first service

If you haven't added any services yet, the Services page shows a "What services do you offer?" box right on the page — you don't need to click anything extra to open it. If you already have at least one service, skip to [Adding another service](#adding-another-service) below, which opens the same form in a pop-up window.

<Steps>
<Step>
Tap one of the **category** tiles to get started: Hair, Makeup, Nails, Skin Care, Beard, Massage, Waxing, or Other. Each tile has an icon so it's easy to scan. Picking a category also fills in a suggested service name (for example, picking Hair suggests "Haircut") that you can change.
</Step>
<Step>
Type the **service name** — for example, "Classic Haircut" or "Balayage." This field is required.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the **duration** — how many minutes the service takes. Tap one of the quick options (15, 30, 45, 60, or 90 minutes), or type a custom number of minutes. This matters: it controls how appointment slots are laid out on your booking calendar, so an inaccurate duration can lead to back-to-back bookings that run over.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the **price** and pick the **currency**. If you'd rather not show a fixed price (for example, for a consultation that's quoted case-by-case), leave the price field empty — customers will see "Pay as you go" instead. If you also leave duration unset, the booking calendar treats the service as 30 minutes by default.
</Step>
<Step>
Tap **Description & staff assignment** to open two optional fields: a short **description** customers see when booking (for example, "Shampoo, cut, and blow-dry"), and a grid of **Assign Staff** tiles where you tap each team member who can perform this service.
</Step>
<Step>
Tap **Add Service →** to save. The new service appears immediately in your list and is available for customers to book right away.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
Assigning staff to a service is optional, not required. If you don't tap any names in **Assign Staff**, the service is open to every staff member who is active and available for booking — assigning specific people *narrows* who can be offered for that service, it doesn't gate whether the service is bookable at all.
</Callout>

## Adding another service

Once you have at least one service, the page shows your list instead of the onboarding box, with an **Add Service** button at the bottom of the list. Tapping it opens the same category-tiles → name/duration/price → description & staff flow described above, in a pop-up window. Fill it in the same way and tap **Add Service →** to save.

## Editing a service

1. On the **Services** page, find the service in your list and tap **Edit** next to it.
2. In the edit window you'll see every field in one place: **Name**, **Description**, **Category** (a dropdown this time, instead of tiles), **Price**, **Currency**, **Duration (min)**, and the **Assign Staff** tiles.
3. At the bottom there's an **Active** checkbox — see the callout below for what this does.
4. Tap **Save changes**. Existing bookings for that service are not affected, but any future bookings will use the updated price, duration, and staff list.

<Callout type="warning">
Changing a service's duration doesn't reflow appointments that are already booked — it only affects new bookings going forward. If you significantly shorten or lengthen a service, double-check your calendar for any appointments that might now look tight.
</Callout>

## Pausing a service without deleting it

If a service is seasonal or temporarily unavailable, you don't have to delete it. Edit the service and uncheck the **Active** box, then **Save changes**. An inactive service:

- Shows an **Inactive** badge in your services list, so your team can still see it exists.
- Disappears completely from the public booking page — customers can't select it.
- Keeps all its settings (price, duration, staff assignments) so you can simply check **Active** again later to bring it back.

## Removing a service

1. Find the service you want to remove and tap the delete (trash) icon next to it.
2. Confirm the removal in the pop-up that appears.

Once removed, the service is gone for good — unlike making it **Inactive**, this can't be undone, and the service no longer appears on your public booking page.

<Callout type="info">
Prefer **Inactive** over deleting when you might bring a service back — it keeps your price, duration, and staff assignments ready to go, whereas deleting removes them permanently.
</Callout>

## Tips for a clean service menu

- **Keep durations realistic.** The time you set here is exactly what customers will see blocked off on the calendar — build in any cleanup or prep time your team needs.
- **Use categories consistently.** Grouping similar services under the same category (Hair, Nails, etc.) makes your public booking page easier for customers to scan.
- **Only assign staff if you need to limit who performs a service.** Leaving the Assign Staff tiles empty keeps a service open to your whole bookable team; use assignments when a service genuinely needs a particular skill (for example, only your colorists should be assigned to a balayage service).
