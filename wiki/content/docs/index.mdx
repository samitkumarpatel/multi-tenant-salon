This help center walks you through every part of running your salon day to day —
from first setting up your profile to taking payments at the front desk.

## Where to start

- **New here?** Start with [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started/sign-up-and-onboarding) to
  create your salon and turn on the features you need.
- **Setting your menu and team?** See [Services](/docs/services/service-catalog) and
  [Staff](/docs/staff/managing-your-team).
- **Taking appointments?** See [Bookings & Scheduling](/docs/bookings/managing-bookings).
- **Selling online?** See [Website & AI Chat](/docs/website/your-salon-website),
  [Web Shop](/docs/shop/setting-up-your-shop), and [Memberships](/docs/membership/creating-membership-plans).
- **Getting paid?** See [Payments](/docs/payments/connecting-your-stripe-account).
- **Running the front desk day to day?** See [Front Desk (Dashboard)](/docs/front-desk/what-is-the-front-desk-app).
- **An individual staff member?** See [Staff Portal](/docs/staff-portal/logging-in-and-your-schedule).
- **Curious about reviews and business data?** See [Reviews & Analytics](/docs/insights/customer-ratings-and-reviews).

<Callout type="info">
  Didn't find what you're looking for? Reach out to support and we'll help you out.
</Callout>
