The Analytics dashboard gives you a simple picture of how people are using your salon's public
website — how many visits it's getting, which pages are popular, and what people are clicking on.

## Turning on Analytics

Analytics is a feature that needs to be switched on for your salon before any data is collected
or shown.

<Callout type="info">
Ask whoever manages your salon's feature settings to enable the **Business analytics dashboard**
feature for your salon. Until it's turned on, no visitor activity is recorded, and the Analytics
page in your admin area will simply tell you it isn't available.
</Callout>

Once it's turned on, your salon's public website automatically starts quietly recording activity
— there's nothing extra for you to set up.

## What gets tracked

While Analytics is enabled, your salon's public website keeps track of:

- **Page views** — every time someone visits a page on your site
- **Clicks** — when someone clicks on a tracked button or link, such as a "Book appointment" button

## Viewing your Analytics summary

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to your salon admin area and open the **Analytics** page.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose a look-back window — by default you'll see the last **7 days**, but you can widen it up to
the last **90 days**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Your summary includes:

- **Page views** and **Clicks tracked** — totals across your chosen time window
- **Visits over time** — a day-by-day chart so you can spot trends (e.g. a spike after a
  promotion)
- **Top pages** — the pages on your website getting the most visits
- **Top clicks** — the specific buttons or links getting clicked most often, such as your "Book
  now" button, so you can see what's drawing customers in

<Callout type="warning">
If Analytics isn't enabled for your salon, the Analytics page will show that access isn't
available rather than any numbers. Turn the feature on first, then give it a little time to
start collecting activity before checking back.
</Callout>

## AI Receptionist chat

Further down the same Analytics page is a separate **AI Receptionist chat** section, covering how
visitors use the AI assistant on your website and booking page. It uses the same look-back window
you chose above, and only appears once the same **Business analytics dashboard** feature is
turned on. It includes:

- **Chat sessions** — how many separate conversations visitors started
- **Messages sent** — how many messages were sent in total across those conversations
- **Bookings proposed via chat** — how many times the assistant put together a booking proposal
  for a visitor
- **Top components shown** — which kinds of cards or pickers the assistant displayed most often
  (for example a services list, a staff profile card, or a date/time picker)
- **Top tools invoked** — what the assistant looked up most often behind the scenes (for example
  checking availability, or looking up services, staff, or holidays)
