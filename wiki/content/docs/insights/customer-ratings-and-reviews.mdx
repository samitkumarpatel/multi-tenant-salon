After a customer's appointment is finished, they get a chance to rate their experience. Here's
how it works and where to find the results.

## How a customer leaves a review

<Steps>
<Step>
Once you (or your team) mark a booking as **completed**, and the customer left an email address
when booking, they automatically receive an email letting them know their appointment is marked
complete. That same email includes a line asking "How was your visit?" with a link to rate the
salon and their stylist.
</Step>
<Step>
The customer clicks that link and is taken to a simple review page showing your salon's name and
the name of the staff member who served them.
</Step>
<Step>
They give two separate star ratings (from 1 to 5): one for the **salon** overall, and one for the
**staff member** who did the work.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
The review link is personal to that one appointment and expires after 30 days. A customer can
only submit one review per appointment — the link simply shows that they've already reviewed it
if they try to use it again.
</Callout>

## Where you see the ratings

Your salon's ratings are shown on your salon's public-facing rating summary, which includes:

- An overall **salon rating** — the average score (out of 5) across every review your salon has
  received, plus how many reviews that's based on.
- A **rating per staff member** — the average score and review count for each individual staff
  member, based only on the reviews where they were the assigned staff member for that booking.

If a staff member or your salon has no reviews yet, no average is shown (there's nothing to
average) — only once reviews start coming in will a score appear.

## Things to keep in mind

- A review is only ever requested for a **completed** booking with a customer email on file — if
  a booking has no email, or is cancelled instead of completed, no review invitation is sent for
  it.
- Ratings are always tied to a specific completed appointment and the staff member assigned to
  it — a customer can't leave a general, unattached review.
- Each completed appointment can only generate one review invitation and one submitted review,
  so your rating numbers reflect actual completed visits rather than repeat voting on the same
  appointment.
