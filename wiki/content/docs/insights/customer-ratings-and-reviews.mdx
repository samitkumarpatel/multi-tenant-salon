---
title: "Customer Ratings & Reviews"
description: "Collect private feedback and star ratings, control website links, and reply to customers."
---

Customers can rate the salon, a specific stylist, or both. They can send stars from 1 to 5,
written feedback, or both. Written feedback and salon replies are private; stars contribute to
the public salon and stylist averages. An unrated category does not count as a zero.

## After a completed appointment

When a booking is marked **Completed**, a customer with an email address receives a notification
with a personal review link. It identifies the salon and the stylist from that appointment,
expires after 30 days, and accepts one submission per appointment. Marking a booking completed
again does not send another invitation. Actual email sending requires the notification provider
to be configured.

Customers can also leave feedback on completed appointments in **My account** and read the
salon's reply there. Completed-visit reviews are tagged **Notification** or **Customer account**
according to where they were submitted.

## Website feedback

The public website has a compact **Leave feedback** link near the footer's contact details. Stylist
cards also offer **Rate this stylist**, which opens the feedback form with that stylist selected.
These links work without account sign-in or an online booking, so walk-in customers can leave
feedback too. The same footer link appears on the shop, membership, loyalty, and account pages.

In **Salon admin → Feedback ratings**, use **Public website feedback** to enable or disable these
website links. Turning it off also blocks website submissions through an already-open form.
It does not disable completed-visit email links, notification feedback, or account reviews.

General feedback is tagged **Website footer**, **Stylist card**, **Public website**, or
**Notification**. These source tags describe entry points; general feedback is not proof of a
completed appointment. Older feedback is labelled **Earlier feedback (source unknown)**.

After sending general feedback, customers can return to the feedback page in the same browser
to see their submission and the salon's reply. This uses a private receipt saved on that browser;
clearing browser storage removes access to that receipt. General feedback is not automatically
attached to an account.

## Other invitations

Customer emails, including fulfilled-order updates and requested till receipts, contain a direct
feedback link. The form works on the booking app even if the salon has no public website.
Booking-completion emails use the appointment's personal review link instead.

## Reading and replying

Open **Salon admin → Feedback ratings** to see feedback from every source. Filter by stylist,
source, or reply status, or search by customer name and service. Entries distinguish **Completed
visit** reviews from **General feedback**. The list includes all entries, with pages of 25.

Use **Reply** to send a private salon response, or **Edit reply** to correct it. Customer stars
and feedback cannot be edited through this screen. Customers read completed-visit replies in
My account and general-feedback replies through their private browser receipt. Replies do not
send an additional email notification.

Staff can read feedback attributed to them and the salon's replies in their own staff portal.
They cannot reply through the staff portal or access another stylist's feedback.
