Once your shop is set up, customers can check out and place orders. This guide explains what
happens the moment an order comes in, how to use the **Orders** tab to work through them, and what
you'll find on an individual order's screen.

## What happens when a customer checks out

When a customer completes checkout:

- Your admin dashboard double-checks that every item they ordered is still available.
- Stock is deducted from each variant's quantity on hand immediately, at the moment of checkout —
  not later. This protects you from overselling.
- If online payment is turned on for your shop (set up in the **Payments** tab), the customer is
  sent to a secure payment page to complete their card payment before the order is confirmed. If a
  payment fails or is abandoned, the stock that was set aside for it is put back automatically.
- If you don't charge online for the shop, the order is confirmed right away and marked as paid.
- A new order appears in your **Orders** tab, and the customer receives a confirmation email.
- The **Orders** tab in the Shop navigation shows a small red count badge for every order that's
  still brand new and hasn't been looked at yet.

## The Orders list

Open the **Orders** tab under Shop to see every order that's come in. Across the top of the list
you can:

- **Search orders…** by order number, customer name, email, phone number, or product name.
- Open the **Status** dropdown to filter to a single status (or **All orders**) — each status in
  the dropdown shows a count next to it, so you can see at a glance how many orders are sitting in
  each stage.
- Choose **Newest first** or **Oldest first** from the sort dropdown.
- Narrow the list to a date range with the **From** and **To** date pickers.
- Click **Clear** to reset every filter back to default once you've applied one.

The list itself shows, for each order: the **Order** number, the **Date** it was placed, the
**Customer** (name and email), the number of **Items**, the order **Total**, and its **Status**
badge, with a **View** link to open it. At the bottom, you can page through with **Prev** and
**Next** once there are more orders than fit on one page.

Click anywhere on an order's row (or its **View** link) to open the order's own detail screen.

## Inside an order

The order detail screen is laid out top to bottom like this:

### Order items

Every line the customer bought, with its product name, variant (e.g. "250 ml"), unit price times
quantity, and line total. Next to each item is a small bell button you can click to notify the
customer about that specific item — more on this below. Under the items, the **Subtotal** and
**Total** are shown.

### Customer and Ship to

Two side-by-side cards: the customer's name with clickable email and phone links (or "No phone" if
none was given), and the shipping address if the order is a delivery (or "No address provided" if
it isn't).

### Actions

This card groups every action you can take on the order, by purpose:

- **Order Status** — buttons appear here depending on the order's current status: **Confirm**,
  **Mark Processing**, **Ready to Ship**, **Mark Shipped**, **Mark Delivered**, **Mark Fulfilled**,
  **Put on Hold**, and **Cancel**. Only the moves that make sense from where the order currently
  is are shown — for example, you can't mark an order "Shipped" before it's been through
  "Processing". Once an order is **Fulfilled**, **Cancelled**, or **Failed**, this group disappears
  entirely, because there's nothing further to move it through.
- **Fulfillment** — an **Update Shipping** button (it relabels itself to show the carrier and
  tracking number once you've saved one) and, once the order is **Delivered** or **Fulfilled**
  and doesn't already have one, a **Request Return** button. Saving shipping details also moves
  the order to **Shipped** if it isn't already. On a finished order the button still shows the
  tracking you saved, but you can no longer change it.
- **Communication** — **Notify Customer** (a free-text message) and **Issue Invoice**.
- **Financial** — **Refunds** (with a red count badge if one is waiting on you), and, once the
  order has a credit note, a **Credit Note _[reference]_** button.

### Order status

The order's lifecycle runs, in order:

**New → Confirmed → Processing → Ready to Ship → Shipped → Delivered → Fulfilled**

with **Cancelled**, **Failed**, and **On Hold** as side states an order can land in instead. You
move an order forward using the matching button in the Order Status action group — for example,
clicking **Mark Processing** once you start preparing it, or **Mark Fulfilled** once the customer
has received it and there's nothing left to do.

**Fulfilled**, **Cancelled** and **Failed** are final. Once an order reaches one of them it can't
be moved back to an earlier status — those orders are settled, and reopening one would contradict
the stock, refund and email history already recorded against it. If goods come back after an order
is finished, use a **return** rather than trying to reopen the order.

<Callout type="info">
  **Cancelling an order puts its items back in stock automatically.** The quantity the customer's
  checkout took off the shelf is credited straight back, so you don't need to correct anything on
  the Inventory screen. The same happens when you mark a return as **Received** or **Accepted** —
  and it only ever happens once per order, so stock can't be double-counted.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Changing an order's status, sending a notification, saving shipping details, and every refund or
  return action are all logged automatically on the order's activity timeline, so you (and anyone
  else on your team) can always see exactly what happened and when.
</Callout>

### Notifying the customer

Clicking **Notify Customer**, or the bell icon on a single item, opens a message box where you
type a free-text update; it's sent to the customer's email (and shows their phone number too, if
you have one on file, for reference). Saving shipping details in the **Update Shipping** window
(choosing a **Carrier** from the dropdown and typing a **Tracking number**, then **Save & notify**)
also emails the customer automatically.

### The activity timeline

Below the actions, every order keeps a running timeline of everything that's happened to it, each
entry with its own icon color and a relative time ("3m ago", "2h ago", and so on). You'll typically
see entries such as:

- **Order placed** — logged automatically the moment the order was placed.
- **Status updated** — logged automatically whenever you move the order to a new status.
- Entries for **Invoice sent**, **Shipment dispatched**, and every **Refund**, **Return**, and
  **Credit** step described in
  [Refunds, Returns, and Credit Notes](/docs/shop/refunds-returns-and-credit-notes).
- **Customer notified** — any entry that went out as an email to the customer carries a
  "Notified" tag; click **View email** on one of these to see exactly who it went to, its subject,
  and its full body, right there in the timeline.
- **Internal note** — a note you add yourself, visible only to you and your team, never the
  customer.

At the bottom of the timeline there's a text box to **Add note** — use it to jot things down for
your own records, like "waiting on a replacement from the supplier" or "customer asked to delay
shipping." This gives you a complete, order-by-order history from the moment it was placed to the
moment it's fulfilled — handy for answering a customer's "what's happening with my order?"
question, or for your own records if a dispute comes up later.
