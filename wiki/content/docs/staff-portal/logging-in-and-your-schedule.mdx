---
title: "Logging In & Your Schedule"
description: "How to sign in to your Staff Portal, check your upcoming appointments, and keep your availability up to date."
---

Your Staff Portal is your own private space to see your appointments, update your profile, and
let your salon know when you're off. This guide walks you through signing in and keeping your
schedule current.

## Signing in

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the Staff Portal sign-in page. You'll see a **Sign in to Staff Portal** screen with a button
that says **Sign In**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Sign In**. You'll be sent to a secure sign-in page to confirm who you are, and then
brought straight back to the Staff Portal.
</Step>
<Step>
If your email is linked to staff accounts at more than one salon, you'll land on a **Select your
account** screen listing every salon you work at. Tap the one you want to open. (If you only have
one account, you're taken straight into the portal — there's nothing to pick.)
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
You can always switch between your salons later, from inside the portal, using the salon name
shown in the top bar — click it and pick a different salon from the list, no need to sign out
first.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
While you're signed in, the portal quietly keeps your session alive in the background — you may
briefly see a "Session renewed" notice near the top of the screen. If your session has fully
expired (for example, you left the portal open for a very long time), you'll simply be asked to
sign in again.
</Callout>

## Finding your way around

Once you're signed in you'll land on your **Dashboard**. The menu on the left (tap the menu icon
in the top-left corner on a phone) gives you quick access to:

- **Dashboard** — your portal's home screen
- **My Profile** — your name, phone number, bio, specializations, profile photo, and your
  availability toggle
- **My Work Media** — photos and videos of your work shown on your salon's public website
- **My Appointments** — every booking assigned to you
- **My Holidays** — the days you've blocked off

The top bar also shows your name and role, and a **Sign out** button you can use from either the
top bar or the bottom of the side menu.

## Viewing your upcoming appointments

Go to **My Appointments** in the side menu. This page lists every booking that's been assigned to
you, across all dates and statuses, newest first, so you can see what's coming up as well as what's
already happened. Each entry shows the customer's name, the date and time, their email (and phone
number and any booking notes, if they left any), and a colored status tag: **Pending**,
**Confirmed**, **Completed**, **Cancelled**, or **No-show**.

Use the row of filter buttons above the list — **All**, **Pending**, **Confirmed**, **Completed**,
**Cancelled**, **No-show** — to narrow the list down to just one status at a time.

## Setting your availability

There are two ways to control when customers can book you:

### Turn booking on or off

Open **My Profile**, click **Edit**, and find the **Available for booking** switch near the
bottom of the edit form. Turning it off tells the salon's booking system that customers currently
cannot book you at all (useful if you're off rotation for a while) — the form says "This staff
member will not appear in the booking calendar." Turn it back on whenever you're ready to take
appointments again, and click **Save changes**. See
[Your Profile & Work Gallery](/en/docs/staff-portal/your-profile-and-work-gallery) for the full
walkthrough of the edit form.

### Book specific days off

For individual days or a longer break, use **My Holidays** in the side menu. It lists your blocked
days under two headings, **Upcoming** and **Past**, each showing the date and your reason (if you
gave one), with a trash-can icon next to every entry so you can remove it at any time.

<Steps>
<Step>
On the **My Holidays** page, click **Book day off** (top-right of the "My days off" card). A
**Book a day off** window opens.
</Step>
<Step>
At the top of the window, choose **Single day** (the default) for one date, or **Date range** to
block off several days in a row (for example, a week of annual leave).
</Step>
<Step>
For a single day, pick the **Date**. For a date range, pick a **Start** date and an **End** date —
as soon as both are set, the form tells you how many days will be booked off.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally fill in **Reason** (it's marked optional) — for example "Annual leave" or "Personal
day" — so your manager knows why you're out.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Book day off** at the bottom of the window to save it. The date(s) immediately disappear
from your available booking slots, so customers can no longer book you on those days.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
Booking a day off does not automatically cancel any appointments you already have on that date.
If you already have bookings on a day you're now taking off, let your manager know so they can
reschedule your customers.
</Callout>

You can remove any day off — upcoming or past — at any time from the **My Holidays** page by
clicking the trash-can icon next to it; removing an upcoming day off immediately makes you
bookable again on that date.

## Your profile and work gallery

**My Profile** and **My Work Media** cover your display name, phone number, bio, specializations,
profile photo, and the gallery of work photos/videos shown on your salon's public website. These
are documented in detail in
[Your Profile & Work Gallery](/en/docs/staff-portal/your-profile-and-work-gallery).

Also on **My Profile**, you can update your display name, phone number, specializations (the
services/skills you're known for), your profile photo, a short bio, and a gallery of your work —
all of which can appear on your salon's public website. Your email address, role, and account
status are managed by your salon's admin and can't be changed from here.
