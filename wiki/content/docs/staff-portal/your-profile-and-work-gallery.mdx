---

Your profile is what your salon (and, through your bio, photo and work gallery, potentially your
salon's public website) sees about you. This page walks through every field on **My Profile** and
**My Work Media**.

## Viewing your profile

Open **My Profile** in the side menu. At the top is your staff card, showing your photo (or a
placeholder icon if you haven't set one), your name, an **Owner** badge if you're the salon's
owner, your role (e.g. "Stylist"), your status (e.g. "Active"), and a "Not bookable" label if
**Available for booking** is currently switched off. Below that it lists your email, phone,
role, status, and the month and year you joined. If you've written a bio ("About me") or added
work photos, they're shown further down, with a **Manage** link that takes you to **My Work
Media**.

<Callout type="info">
Your email, role, and status are managed by your salon's admin and can't be changed from here.
If you need any of these changed, reach out to your salon manager directly.
</Callout>

## Editing your profile

<Steps>
<Step>
On **My Profile**, click **Edit** (top-right of the staff card). An **Edit Staff** window opens.
</Step>
<Step>
At the top is your photo. Click the circular photo area to **upload** a photo from your device,
or click the small camera button on its bottom-right corner to **take a photo** using your
device's camera instead (see below). If you already have a photo, a small X button in the
top-right corner of the photo lets you remove it.
</Step>
<Step>
Fill in **Name** (required).
</Step>
<Step>
**Email** is shown but greyed out — it can't be edited here.
</Step>
<Step>
Fill in **Phone** using the phone number field, and note that **Role** is shown but greyed out —
it can't be edited here either.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Specializations**, tap each skill that applies to you — Hair, Makeup, Nails, Skin Care,
Beard, Massage, Waxing, Other — to select or deselect it.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **About me**, write a short introduction shown on the salon website. There's a **Fix with
AI** button above this field — see "Polishing your bio with AI" below.
</Step>
<Step>
**Status** is shown but greyed out — it can't be edited here.
</Step>
<Step>
Further down, switch the **Available for booking** toggle on or off. The helper text underneath
changes to confirm what it means: on means "Customers can book appointments with this staff
member," off means "This staff member will not appear in the booking calendar."
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save changes** to save everything, or **Cancel** to close the window without saving.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
The edit window doesn't let you manage your work photos/videos gallery — there's a reminder line
under "About me" pointing you to the separate **My Work Media** page for that, covered below.
</Callout>

### Taking or uploading a profile photo

When you click the small camera button, a **Take a photo** window opens using your device's
camera:

<Steps>
<Step>
Allow camera access if your browser asks for it. Once the camera feed appears, your photo lines
up inside a circular guide.
</Step>
<Step>
If your device has more than one camera, click the flip-camera button in the top-right corner to
switch between your front and back camera.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the large round shutter button to capture the photo.
</Step>
</Steps>

Your captured (or uploaded) photo is only saved to your profile once you click **Save changes**
in the Edit Staff window.

### Polishing your bio with AI

Next to the **About me** field is a **Fix with AI** button. Clicking it offers a few rewritten
versions of whatever you've typed, styled differently from each other; you can preview each one,
click **Use this text** to replace your draft with the one you like, or **Dismiss** to keep what
you originally wrote. Editing the text yourself cancels any suggestions still on screen. The
button is disabled if the field is empty.

## Managing your work gallery

Go to **My Work Media** in the side menu. This page is only for your gallery of work photos and
videos (your profile photo/avatar is set on the **My Profile** page instead) — it shows how many
items you have out of a maximum of **12**, and every change here saves automatically, with no
separate save button.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add media** (or, if your gallery is empty, the large dashed box that says "Add your first
photo or video").
</Step>
<Step>
Choose one or more photo or video files from your device. Accepted formats are JPG, PNG, WebP,
MP4, and WebM, and each file can be up to 25 MB.
</Step>
<Step>
Each file uploads and is added to your gallery automatically — no extra confirmation step.
</Step>
</Steps>

Each item in the gallery shows a small **Video** tag in its bottom-left corner if it's a video, so
you can tell images and videos apart at a glance. To remove an item, click the small X button in
its top-right corner — this also saves immediately.

<Callout type="warning">
You can't add more items once you reach 12. Remove something first if you want to add new work
samples.
</Callout>
