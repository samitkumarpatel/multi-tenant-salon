---
title: "Managing Your Team"
description: "How to add, edit, and remove staff members on your salon's roster."
---

The **Staff** page in your admin panel is where you manage everyone who works at your salon — their role, status, and the services they specialize in. Staff members can be assigned to specific services so customers can book appointments with them.

<Callout type="info">
When you haven't added anyone yet, the **Add Team Member** form appears directly on the page under "Who's on your team?". Once you have at least one team member, your team is shown as a list instead, and adding someone new opens a popup window with the same form.
</Callout>

## Adding a team member

1. Click **Add Team Member**.
2. Fill in their **Full name** (required).
3. Pick their **Role** — **Manager**, **Stylist**, **Receptionist**, or **Assistant**.
4. Fill in their **Email** (required).
5. Click **Phone, specializations, work media & availability** to expand more optional fields:
   - A **profile photo** — upload one, or take one directly with your camera
   - **Phone number**
   - **Specializations** — the types of services they're skilled in: Hair, Makeup, Nails, Skin Care, Beard, Massage, Waxing, or Other
   - **About me** — a short bio shown on your public website. Next to this field is a **Fix with AI** button: write a rough draft, click it, and choose between three improved versions (polished, friendly, or concise) to use, or dismiss them and keep your own wording.
   - A **work gallery** — photos or short videos of their work, shown on your public website
   - **Calendar availability** — their weekly schedule. It's pre-filled from your salon's opening hours, and you can't set a day's hours outside your salon's own operating hours for that day.
6. Click **Add Team Member →** to save.

New team members are set to **Active** automatically, so they're immediately available to be assigned bookings.

<Callout type="info">
If you've turned on Booking and have one or fewer team members, you'll see a reminder banner on the Staff page encouraging you to add more — you can dismiss it.
</Callout>

## Editing a team member

Hover over (or tap) a team member's row and click **Edit** to open their details in a popup window. You can update:

- **Photo, name, email, phone, role**
- **Specializations**, **About me** (with the same **Fix with AI** helper), and work gallery
- **Status** — Active, Inactive, or On Leave
- **Available for booking** — turn this off to keep someone on your roster without customers being able to book appointments with them; they simply won't appear in the booking calendar

Click **Save changes** to apply your updates, or **Cancel** to close without saving.

## Removing a team member

Hover over (or tap) a team member's row and click the trash icon, then confirm by clicking **Remove** in the popup that appears. This deletes them from your team list.

<Callout type="info">
The salon owner can't be removed from the team list — there's no remove button next to their name (shown with an **Owner** badge).
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
Removing a staff member, or marking them **Inactive**, immediately revokes their access to the front-desk dashboard app — they're checked against your active roster on every sign-in attempt, so there's no delay in cutting off access.
</Callout>
